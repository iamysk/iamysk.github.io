## Background & Research

I am a **Senior Machine Learning Scientist at [Elucidata](https://elucidata.io/)**, developing AI/ML workflows for biomedical data, structural modeling, and drug discovery.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral fellow supported by the **Wenner-Gren Foundation** in the [Bioinformatics Division (BIOIN)](https://liu.se/en/organisation/liu/ifm/bioin) at [Linköping University, Sweden](https://liu.se/), working with [Prof. Björn Wallner](https://liu.se/en/employee/bjowa51). Our work focused on computational modeling of protein conformational landscapes, leading to tools such as [AFsample2](https://github.com/iamysk/AFsample2) (*Communications Biology*, 2025) and [AFsample3](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.01.16.699904) (*bioRxiv*, 2026) for predicting dynamic conformational states and structural ensembles with AlphaFold.

I completed my Ph.D. at the [Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (IIT Delhi)](https://home.iitd.ac.in/) in 2023 under the supervision of [Prof. Durai Sundar](https://web.iitd.ac.in/~sundar/index.html). My doctoral research developed deep learning methods that integrate multi-omics datasets for clinical prognosis modeling and drug-target interaction (DTI) discovery—including [TransDTI](https://github.com/iamysk/TransDTI) (*ACS Omega*, 2022) and [SurvCNN](https://doi.org/10.3390/cancers13133106) (*Cancers*, 2021).

Prior to IIT Delhi, I earned my **Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.) in Biotechnology** from **GB Pant University of Agriculture and Technology (GBPUAT), Uttarakhand, India**, where I conducted undergraduate research with [Prof. Sanjeev Agarwal](https://gbpuat.ac.in/colleges/CBSH/D1/sanjeev_profile.html) studying antimicrobial properties of steroidal lactones from *Withania somnifera*.
